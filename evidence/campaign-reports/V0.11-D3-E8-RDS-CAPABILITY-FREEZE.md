## 5. Reopen gate（唯一重开条件，机械化）

> RDS online qualification may reopen only if natural workload
> mechanism exposure materially increases to an effect scale that
> a preregistered power analysis says is measurable.

机械化：任何新 opportunity/implementation 必须先通过**离线 exposure
census**，证明预计 task-relative mechanism exposure 达到**至少数个
百分点量级**，然后重新执行 power design（E7 方法、新 prereg）。
"10-30×" 是当前 workload 与噪声下的量级推导，**不是冻结的普适
常数**。禁止"想再试试"就重跑 120 sessions。
